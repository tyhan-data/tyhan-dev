I'm a Computer Science & Technology student working toward becoming a Machine Learning Engineer. Most of my time goes into building projects with Python and the usual ML stack, then figuring out how to make them run somewhere other than my own machine.

My learning path started with traditional machine learning, moved into deep learning, and then pulled me toward the engineering side: APIs, containers, and deployment. The part I enjoy most is taking a trained model and wrapping it into something a person can open in a browser and use.

Right now I'm focused on backend systems, model serving, Docker, and production-oriented ML workflows — the gap between a good notebook and a working application.
